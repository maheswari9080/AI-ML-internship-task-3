🌱 Excited to share my latest AI/ML Internship project: **Smart Waste Classification System (Task 3)**! 🚀

Improper waste segregation remains one of the largest hurdles in recycling and environmental sustainability. For this project, I developed an end-to-end Deep Learning Computer Vision pipeline to classify recyclable waste into three primary streams:

🧴 **Plastic** (Bottles, containers, jugs)
📰 **Paper** (Cardboard, packaging, newspapers)
🥫 **Metal** (Soda cans, food tins, scrap aluminum)

---

### 🔑 Key Project Highlights:

📊 **Curated Custom Dataset (105 Samples)**:
Collected and balanced a multi-class dataset of 105 images, rigorously capturing diverse conditions including varied camera angles (tilted/45°/top-down), distances (close-up vs full-frame), lighting environments, and complex real-world backgrounds.

🧠 **Deep Learning Transfer Learning**:
Trained and exported a MobileNet / Keras neural network model (`keras_model.h5`), achieving fast inference latency (<45ms) suitable for edge computing and smart IoT devices.

💻 **Interactive Web & Webcam Studio**:
Built an in-browser studio (`mw_dataset_viewer.html`) featuring:
• Complete dataset visualization gallery
• Real-time webcam capture with burst mode (5x)
• Dynamic variation checklist ensuring high data quality
• 1-click batch image export

🔄 **Real-World Integration**:
Designed a full architecture pipeline for smart recycling bins, automated conveyor belts, and municipal waste sorting facilities.

---

💡 **Takeaway**:
This task demonstrated how critical data quality, diverse angles, and lighting considerations are in deploying practical Computer Vision models that perform reliably outside controlled environments.
